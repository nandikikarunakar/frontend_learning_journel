Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

President of India

Official portrait, 1962 President of India In office 13 May 1962 – 13 May 1967

Remembered as a philosopher‑statesman who bridged Eastern and Western thought, and as an inspiring teacher who believed “teachers should be the best minds in the country.”

His birthday, September 5, is celebrated as Teachers’ Day in India to honor his dedication to education

Some facts about him:

  • He was born on September 5, 1888, in Tiruttani, Tamil Nadu.
  • He studied philosophy at Madras Christian College, graduating with honors.
  • He was a renowned interpreter of Advaita Vedanta and bridged Eastern and Western philosophy.
  • He taught at Madras Presidency College, University of Mysore, University of Calcutta, and even at Oxford University.
  • He served as Vice-Chancellor of Andhra University and Banaras Hindu University.
  • He became the first Vice President of India (1952–1962) and later the second President of India (1962–1967).
  • His birthday, September 5, is celebrated as Teachers’ Day in India to honor his dedication to education.
  • He received the Bharat Ratna in 1954, India’s highest civilian honor.
  • He passed away on April 17, 1975, in Chennai.
