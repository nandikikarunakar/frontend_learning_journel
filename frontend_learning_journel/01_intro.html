BMW(Bayerische Motoren Werke AG, or Bavarian Motor Works) is a renowned German German multinational corporation that produces luxury vehicles and motorcycles. Key Points About BMW

  • Founded: 7 March 1916 in Munich, Germany.
  • Founders: Franz Josef Popp, Karl Rapp, Gustav Otto, Max Friz, Camillo Castiglioni
  • Industry: Automotive (luxury cars, motorcycles, financial services)

Brands & Products

  1. Automobiles: BMW (i, M, X, Z, Alpina), MINI, Rolls-Royce.
  2. Motorcycles: BMW Motorrad.
  3. Car-sharing, financing, leasing, insurance.
